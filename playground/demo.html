Arms and the man I sing, who, forced by fate and haughty Juno's unrelenting hate, expelled and exiled, left the Trojan shore. Long labours, both by sea and land, he bore.

Midway upon the journey of our life I found myself within a forest dark, for the straightforward pathway had been lost.

Night had fallen over the quay, and the lamps burned low along the water, each one doubled in the black of the harbour and drawn out by the swell.

Snow began to fall at dusk, thin and unhurried, over a town already asleep and long past caring what the weather chose to do with the night.
